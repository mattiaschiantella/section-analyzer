About this tool

    

      Section Analyzer is a free educational calculator for cross-sections built from rectangles.
      Define the rectangles by their top-right vertex, base and height (in mm), compute the geometry,
      then apply internal actions to see the stress maps. Everything runs in your browser:
      no data is sent to any server.
    


    
What it computes

    
    
      
  • Geometric properties: area, centroid, second moments of area Ix, Iy, mean inertia and principal axes.
    • 
      
  • Normal stress: σ from axial force N and biaxial bending Mx, My, with the maximum tension and compression and where they occur.
    • 
      
  • Shear stress: approximate Jourawski method, τ = T·S / (I·b) (T = shear force, S = static moment, b = wall thickness), evaluated separately for Tx and Ty piece by piece along the wall of the section.
    • 
      
  • Torsional shear stress: open thin-walled sections (Saint-Venant), J = Σ L·b³ / 3, τt,max = Mz·b / J.
    • 
    


    
Conventions

    
    
      
  • Reference system: x positive to the left, y positive downward (De Saint Venant's convention in structural mechanics).
    • 
      
  • Lengths in mm, forces in kN, moments in kN·m, stresses in MPa.
    • 
      
  • The torsional moment Mz is positive if counterclockwise.
    • 
    


    

      Limits of the method. The shear and torsion results assume thin-walled open sections made of
      contiguous rectangles. Closed sections (boxes, cells) are not handled correctly for shear, and for thick
      pieces (e.g. a square) loaded perpendicular to their long side the shear through the thickness is neglected.
      Overlapping rectangles are counted twice. Use the results as an educational estimate and check critical cases independently.

        Section Analyzer è un calcolatore gratuito, a scopo didattico, per sezioni composte da rettangoli contigui:
        area, baricentro, momenti d'inerzia e assi principali, tensioni normali da sforzo normale e flessione deviata,
